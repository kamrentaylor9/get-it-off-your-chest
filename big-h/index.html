Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out to the actual site, look at the ground, and give you a clear price at no charge and no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

Jacksonville and the surrounding central Arkansas area — including Cabot, Sherwood, Beebe, Searcy, and nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Just call and ask.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, fully licensed and insured. If anything ever went wrong on your property, you're covered — that protection is part of what you're paying for.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job, but we'll give you a realistic timeline up front. Use the "Timeline" field on the quote form to tell us how soon you need it.

What kind of dirt work do you handle?

Land clearing and grading, house and shop pads, gravel driveways, drainage and culverts, demolition and haul-off, foundation and footing digs, and underground utility trenching. If you're not sure it's in our wheelhouse, ask — we'll tell you straight.

Do I need to know exact measurements?

No. Ballpark is fine to start — and the estimate tools on this page can help you get close. We confirm final quantities when we look at the site.
